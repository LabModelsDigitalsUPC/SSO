Introduction back to ToC

Structural simulation data can be represented within SSO as an RDF/OWL knowledge model, enabling structural analysis information to be semantically represented, queried, and linked with other built-environment information. SSO provides classes and relationships for describing structural simulation models and their connections to physical assets, while also supporting links to external repositories containing complete simulation representations. The ontology is organized around five groups of classes: (1) the **structural model and its mesh** — `StructuralAnalysisModel`, `Object`, `Mesh`, `Node`, `Element` and its `Solid`/`Shell`/`Beam` subclasses; (2) **materials and sections** — `Material`, `ElasticBehaviour`/`PlasticBehaviour`, `Section` and its `Beam`/`Shell`/`Solid` subclasses, and parametric `CrossSectionProfile` shapes; (3) **assembly** — `Assembly`, `Instance`, `Nset`/`Elset`; (4) **actions** — `LoadCase`, `Load` (point, distributed, surface) and `BoundaryCondition`; and (5) **provenance** — `Calibration`, built on `prov:Activity`, for tracking how a parameter's value came to be. See Section 1.1 for the namespaces SSO reuses or links out to.
