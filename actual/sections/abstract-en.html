Abstract

SSO (Structural Simulation Ontology) is an OWL ontology for representing structural analysis models as an RDF graph. It captures the finite element mesh (objects, nodes, elements), its assembled instances and node/element sets, the materials and sections — including parametric cross-section profiles — assigned to elements, and the load cases, loads, and boundary conditions applied to the model. SSO also provides open-domain properties (`referenced_in_sam`, `idealized_by`) for linking a structural analysis model, and the finite elements that idealize a physical component, to descriptions of that same building or bridge in external building/topology ontologies (BOT, BEO, BROT), and a PROV-based `Calibration` class for recording how a model parameter was calibrated against measured data. SSO is developed to support integrating structural simulation data into knowledge-graph-based digital twins. It is based on the O-SAM (Open Structural Analysis Models) data model available at: https://github.com/LabModelsDigitalsUPC/O-SAM
