Abstract

SSO (Structural Simulation Ontology) is an OWL ontology for representing O-SAM (Open Structural Analysis Model) structural simulation models as an RDF graph. It captures a structural analysis model's finite-element mesh (objects, nodes, elements), its assembled instances and node/element sets, the materials and sections — including parametric cross-section profiles — assigned to elements, and the load cases, loads, and boundary conditions applied to the model. SSO also provides open-domain properties (`referenced_in_sam`, `idealized_by`) for linking a structural analysis model, and the finite elements that idealize a physical component, to descriptions of that same building or bridge in external building/topology ontologies (BOT, BEO, BROT), and a PROV-based `Calibration` class for recording how a model parameter was calibrated against measured data. SSO is developed to support integrating structural-simulation data into knowledge-graph-based digital twins.
